Trade terms

Choose how the shipment is shared.

Tell us your preferred trade term in the enquiry, or leave it open and we will advise. Here is a plain-language view of the four terms we list.

Ex Works (EXW)

The seller makes the goods available at its premises. The buyer arranges export clearance, transport, freight and everything after.

Goods made ready
Export clearance and loading
Main sea freight
Import clearance and delivery
Arranged by sellerArranged by buyer

Free On Board (FOB)

The seller clears the goods for export and loads them on the vessel at the port of shipment. The buyer arranges freight and everything after.

Goods made ready
Export clearance and loading
Main sea freight
Import clearance and delivery
Arranged by sellerArranged by buyer

Cost and Freight (CFR)

The seller pays the sea freight to the destination port. Risk passes to the buyer once the goods are loaded on the vessel.

Goods made ready
Export clearance and loading
Main sea freight
Import clearance and delivery
Arranged by sellerArranged by buyer

Cost, Insurance and Freight (CIF)

As CFR, with the seller also arranging minimum insurance to the destination port. Risk still passes to the buyer at loading.

Export clearance and loading
Main sea freight
Import clearance and delivery

Seller also arranges minimum insurance

Simplified guide. The exact obligations follow Incoterms® 2020 and the terms agreed for your order.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What should I include in an enquiry?

Product name, destination country, estimated quantity and packaging preference. A preferred trade term and any specification help us respond faster.

Can specifications be tailored?

Yes. Varieties, grades, packaging, order quantities and availability can be tailored to your market and purchase requirements, subject to availability.

What is the minimum order quantity?

It depends on the product, packaging and shipping method, so we confirm it for each enquiry.

Which documents do you provide?

Documents are prepared for the specific order. The set depends on the product and destination, so tell us your import requirements early.

Which trade terms are available?

EXW, FOB, CFR and CIF, or leave it open and we will advise on the best fit.

Do you work with buyers worldwide?

We work with international buyers. We confirm shipping options for each destination when we prepare your proposal.
