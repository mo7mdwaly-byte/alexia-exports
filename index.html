Process

How an order works

  1. Enquiry

    Send the product, quantity and destination port.

  2. Specification

    We agree variety, size, packing and labels, and send samples if needed.

  3. Quotation

    You receive a price on the terms you choose, such as FOB or CIF.

  4. Packing & loading

    Goods are packed, checked and loaded into the container.

  5. Shipping & documents

    The container sails and the export documents follow.
