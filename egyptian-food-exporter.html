Egyptian Food & Agricultural Products Exporter

From fresh citrus to frozen berries and dry legumes, ALEXIA EXPORTS organises Egyptian food and agricultural products for export.

Product categories we export

Browse the products we supply from Egypt:

  • Citrus & oranges, grapes and seasonal fruits
  • Vegetables & herbs, olives and edible oils
  • Frozen fruits & vegetables, including IQF berries
  • Dates & dried fruits
  • Dry legumes & pulses: chickpeas, white kidney beans and rice
  • Processed foods, dairy & foodstuffs, salt & minerals and food ingredients

The complete list is on our products page.

Who we supply

We work with importers, wholesalers, distributors, packers, food-service buyers and food manufacturers. Quantities, grades and packaging are tailored to the buyer rather than fixed in advance.

Why source from Egypt

  • Long seasons: Navel oranges, Valencia oranges, mandarins, grapes, strawberries and pomegranates are harvested in different months. See the orange season guide.
  • Port access on the Mediterranean and the Red Sea for sea freight to Europe, the Gulf, Africa and Asia.
  • Wide range: fresh, frozen, dry and processed products from one coordinated supplier.
